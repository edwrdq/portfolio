it's cool having a website. my friends have cool sites. i wanted a cool site.

that's the whole reason. the rest is just how it went.

**the look.** i wanted it to feel like a rick owens site, or any fashion brand's: black, raw, quiet. then i mixed in a terminal, since that's where i actually live. one accent color (acid), pixel icons, and seams that don't line up on purpose. my friend's site [kat.wang](https://kat.wang) was a big inspo for the personality side, and for features like the message thingy.

**who built it.** mostly ai, to be fully honest. i did the direction: picked the references, made the images, decided what goes where, and said "no, more rick" a lot. i'm good at knowing what i want. i'm less good at writing astro, or any frontend really. i can imagine a cool site but not write one, and i'm not that interested in learning. not saying it's not important, it's just not my thing. as a future meche major, it makes more sense for me to get really good at something like c++ or assembly.

**the stack.** astro on cloudflare. there's a [guestbook](/#guestbook) on the homepage (leave something), comments on posts, and a "now" thing that shows what i'm listening to. data lives in cloudflare d1, and files and images live in r2.

**the secret half.** there's also a private admin side with tools i made for myself: an idea inbox, a link shortener, file drops, a pixel icon editor, and the dither tool that made every image here. the site is maybe 40% portfolio and 60% me building a little control room for myself.

**what's next.** more writing, a garage section once the civic build starts (so once i have money to spend), and maybe some minigames.

anyways, you're here now. say something in the [guestbook](/#guestbook).
